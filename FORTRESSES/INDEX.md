# MIRATHIA — 8 FORTRESS IDENTITIES

The Notion fortress standard defines eight canonical architectural identities and requires each fortress to be recognizable from name, biome, silhouette, material, light, levels, circulation, function and artefact relationship.

|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Das Schloss der Harmonie | Mirathia | Kreis, Symmetrie, sieben Achsen, zentraler Schlosskern, sieben Tore und sieben Brückenachsen |
| 02 | Der Smaragdthron | Sylvandor | Lebender Herzbaum, Wurzeln, spiralförmige Wege, Stammhallen, Asttürme, Krone |
| 03 | Die Mondgarten-Festung | Lunafall | Ovale Hochlandanlage, Gartenring, Königshof, vier astronomische Türme |

## Binding visual standard

**CMSF — Cinematic Mythic Storybook Fantasy**

- Characters: lightly stylized, expressive and clearly readable.
- Environment: highly detailed cinematic digital painting.
- Light: volumetric, atmospheric and controlled.
- Architecture: luxurious fantasy-book illustration with credible spatial construction.
- Formats: cinematic keyart, God-Tier ArtStation, Ultra-Collector Limited Edition and DIN A4 portrait architecture plate.

## Mirathia gate rule

The seven outer gates of Mirathia are not decorated with generic guardian statues. Each gate contains a paired monumental petrified guardian composition:

**Aureon — Königslöwe of Mirathia + the corresponding regional Urbestie.**

The pair belongs to the gate's regional axis. The regional gate retains the material, ornamentation and light character of its biome while the Mirathia Königslöwe remains the common central guardian.
