# MIRATHIA — MASTER REGISTER DER 8 URBESTIEN

Source basis: current Notion master pages `12. 🐾 Völker & Wesen`, individual Urbestien pages, `3. ✨ Artefakte`, and the `8 BIOME RESONANZ-MATRIX`.

## Binding system rule
Every one of the 8 Biomes has exactly one assigned Urbestie. The Urbestie is bound to the Biome's primary resonance and its Artefact relationship.

For the Mirathia fortress system, the **King Lion Aureon** is the Mirathia Urbestie and is additionally present at every Mirathia outer gate as one half of the monumental petrified guardian pair. At each gate, Aureon stands together with the local Biome's Urbestie. This gate-statue rule is a project-level architectural rule and does not replace the Biome assignment below.

## 01 — MIRATHIA
- Urbestie: **Aureon**
- Species: **Königslöwe**
- Resonance: Licht / Nexus / Harmonie
- Ability: **HERZ DER HARMONIE** — Aureon can temporarily attune different resonances to one another.
- Function: central light anchor; his resonance connects with the other seven Urbestien.
- Artefact: **Die Maske des Mitgefühls**

## 02 — SYLVANDOR
- Urbestie: **Eldarion**
- Species: **Herzbaumreh**
- Resonance: Leben / Regeneration
- Ability: **HERZBAUM-RESONANZ** — Eldarion connects with the Heart Tree and stabilizes Sylvandor's life energy.
- Function: life and regeneration resonance.
- Artefact: **Die Krone des Herzbaums**

## 03 — LUNAfall
- Urbestie: **Selenor**
- Species: **Mondhirsch**
- Resonance: Mondlicht / Hoffnung
- Ability: **MONDRESONANZ** — Selenor concentrates Lunafall's moon/star light and makes hidden paths or visions visible.
- Function: moon / hope resonance.
- Artefact: **Die Träne der Sehnsucht**

## 04 — PYRAKH

- Resonance: Feuer / Magma / Gestein
- Ability: **HERZ DER MAGMA** — Ignarok combines fire, magma and stone into stable protective energy.
- Function: fire / earth resonance.
- Artefact: **Das Gefäß der Großzügigkeit**

## 05 — GLIMMERFURT

## 06 — AQUARELIS

- Species: **Tiefenwyrm**

- Ability: **TIEFENRESONANZ** — Thalassor connects the water resonance of depth, ether column, reefs and ascent.

## 07 — AETHYRS P ITZ
- Urbestie: **Aerion**

- Resonance: Wind / Eis / Höhe / Äther
- Ability: **HIMMELSRESONANZ** — Aerion combines wind, cold, crystal and ether into a stable high-altitude current.

- Urbestie: **Elyssoia**
- Species: **Nebelschleierkatze**

- Critical identity lock: **Elyssoia is a CAT, explicitly not a fox.** The ether fox remains a separate Vesperia signature creature.
- Function: ether / mist / transition resonance.
- Artefact: **Kelch des Friedens**

## Meta-Artefact
The **Sphäre der Harmonie** is not an independent ninth starting artefact. It is created only through the complete union of the eight Biome artefacts / resonance components.

## Continuity locks
- 8 Biomes = 8 Urbestien.
- Aureon is the Mirathia Urbestie and the permanent central reference.
- Gate architecture: Aureon + the local Biome Urbestie, both represented as monumental petrified guardian figures.
- Elyssoia must remain a Nebelschleierkatze; do not replace her with a fox.
- Historical source divergence: the earlier GitHub version listed “Ätherkern” for Vesperia. By binding decision dated 2026-11-13, ART-008 is “Kelch des Friedens”; “Ätherkern” is a historical, non-authoritative divergent entry, not an alias or a second artefact.
- ART-007 binding decision dated 2026-11-13: the artefact is **Spiegel der Demut** (stable ID ART-007). The former GitHub label **Atem des Himmelsdrachen** is a divergent historical source designation, not an alias, renaming, or additional Biome base artefact. The Aethyrspitz Notion artefact dossier and production definition identify the Spiegel der Demut; the older canonical record explicitly states that Atem des Himmelsdrachen is not active. The user confirmed Spiegel der Demut as the binding name.
- ART-004 binding decision dated 2026-11-13 (Variante A): the artefact is **Das Gefäß der Großzügigkeit**. The former label **Lavagestein-Herz** is retained only as a historical, archived WELT-Bau/Biome source designation, not as an alias, renaming, or additional artefact. The current Pyrakh production hierarchy remains authoritative; the conflicting older Pyrak Character Master Bible is documented as an unresolved source discrepancy and is not modified by this synchronization.
- ART-003 binding decision dated 2026-11-13 (Variante A): the artefact is **Träne der Sehnsucht**. The former GitHub label **Sichelmond-Samen** is retained only as a historical divergent source designation, not as an ART-003 alias, renaming, or additional Biome base artefact. Separate Lunafall companion-object source records named “Sichelmond-Samen” remain outside ART-003 and are not changed by this synchronization.
