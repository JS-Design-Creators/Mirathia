# MIRATHIA — MASTER REGISTER DER 8 URBESTIEN

Source basis: current Notion master pages `12. 🐾 Völker & Wesen`, individual Urbestien pages, `3. ✨ Artefakte`, and the `8 BIOME RESONANZ-MATRIX`.

## Binding system rule
Every one of the 8 Biomes has exactly one assigned Urbestie. The Urbestie is bound to the Biome's primary resonance and its Artefact relationship.

For the Mirathia fortress system, the **King Lion Aureon** is the Mirathia Urbestie and is additionally present at every Mirathia outer gate as one half of the monumental petrified guardian pair. At each gate, Aureon stands together with the local Biome's Urbestie. This gate-statue rule is a project-level architectural rule and does not replace the Biome assignment below.

## 01 — MIRATHIA
- Urbestie: **Aureon**
- Species: **Königslöwe**
- Resonance: Licht / Nexus / Harmonie
- Ability: **HERZ DER HARMONIE** — Aureon can temporarily attune different resonances to one another.
- Function: central light anchor; his resonance connects with the other seven Urbestien.
- Artefact: **Die Maske des Mitgefühls**

## 02 — SYLVANDOR
- Urbestie: **Eldarion**
- Species: **Herzbaumreh**
- Resonance: Leben / Regeneration
- Ability: **HERZBAUM-RESONANZ** — Eldarion connects with the Heart Tree and stabilizes Sylvandor's life energy.
- Function: life and regeneration resonance.
- Artefact: **Die Krone des Herzbaums**

## 03 — LUNAfall
- Urbestie: **Selenor**
- Species: **Mondhirsch**
- Resonance: Mondlicht / Hoffnung
- Ability: **MONDRESONANZ** — Selenor concentrates Lunafall's moon/star light and makes hidden paths or visions visible.
- Function: moon / hope resonance.
- Artefact: **Der Sichelmond-Samen**

## 04 — PYRAKH
- Urbestie: **Ignarok**
- Species: **Lava-Gestein-Löwe**
- Resonance: Feuer / Magma / Gestein
- Ability: **HERZ DER MAGMA** — Ignarok combines fire, magma and stone into stable protective energy.
- Function: fire / earth resonance.
- Artefact: **Das Lavagestein-Herz**

## 05 — GLIMMERFURT
- Urbestie: **Mirravi**
- Species: **Prismafuchs**
- Resonance: Licht / Spiegel / Prisma
- Ability: **PRISMARESONANZ** — Mirravi transforms water and light into a living mirror/prism resonance.
- Function: light / mirror resonance.
- Artefact: **Der Schleier des Wanderers**

## 06 — AQUARELIS
- Urbestie: **Thalassor**
- Species: **Tiefenwyrm**
- Resonance: Wasser / Tiefe / Strömung
- Ability: **TIEFENRESONANZ** — Thalassor connects the water resonance of depth, ether column, reefs and ascent.
- Function: water / depth resonance.
- Artefact: **Der Tiefenruf des Triton**

## 07 — AETHYRS P ITZ
- Urbestie: **Aerion**
- Species: **Himmelsgreif**
- Resonance: Wind / Eis / Höhe / Äther
- Ability: **HIMMELSRESONANZ** — Aerion combines wind, cold, crystal and ether into a stable high-altitude current.
- Function: wind / altitude resonance.
- Artefact: **Der Atem des Himmelsdrachen**

## 08 — VESPERIA
- Urbestie: **Elyssia**
- Species: **Nebelschleierkatze**
- Resonance: Äther / Nebel / Übergang
- Ability: **SCHLEIERRESONANZ** — Elyssia connects ether and mist into a living veil capable of hiding and preserving.
- Critical identity lock: **Elyssia is a CAT, explicitly not a fox.** The ether fox remains a separate Vesperia signature creature.
- Function: ether / mist / transition resonance.
- Artefact: **Der Ätherkern**

## Meta-Artefact
The **Sphäre der Harmonie** is not an independent ninth starting artefact. It is created only through the complete union of the eight Biome artefacts / resonance components.

## Continuity locks
- 8 Biomes = 8 Urbestien.
- Aureon is the Mirathia Urbestie and the permanent central reference.
- Gate architecture: Aureon + the local Biome Urbestie, both represented as monumental petrified guardian figures.
- Elyssia must remain a Nebelschleierkatze; do not replace her with a fox.
- Do not invent additional Urbestien.
