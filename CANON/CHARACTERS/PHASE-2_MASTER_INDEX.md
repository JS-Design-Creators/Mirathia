## Integrity note

The canonical group structure above is the current Mirathia structure: 01–08 Light Watchers, 09–11 the three heroes, 12–16 Shadow Warriors.

The repository already contains a complete Phase-2 registration for positions 09–11. The Light Watchers already have their Phase-1 canonical records, Asset-IDs, Visual DNA and Prompt-IDs. Their Phase-2 master registration is therefore recorded here without changing their established identifiers.

The five Shadow Warrior records currently use a legacy numbering namespace in the repository: Malakor is recorded as Asset/Prompt 001, Irdra 002, Morwen 003, Grothak 004 and Voros 005. Those identifiers collide with the canonical 01–08 Light Watcher namespace. They are therefore preserved as legacy identifiers and are **not silently renumbered** in this index.

A final all-16 Phase-2 lock requires an explicit identifier normalization for positions 12–16 so that every character has one unique Asset-ID and Prompt-ID. No new identifiers are invented in this file.
