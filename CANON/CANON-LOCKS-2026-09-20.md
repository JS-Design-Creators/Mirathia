### C-004 — Shadow Lord

`Malkor` is a historical variant only.

### C-005 — Mirathia geography

- Mirathia is the absolute center of the world.
- Seven outer biomes surround Mirathia.
- Seven bridge routes lead from Mirathia to the seven outer biomes.
- The Vesperia bridge rises into the sky because Vesperia is the Flying Crystal Realm.
- A separate **Himmelsbrücke** runs from the highest point of Aethyrspitz upward beyond the clouds to Vesperia.
- The Himmelsbrücke is distinct from the seven ordinary Mirathia bridge routes.

### C-006 — Chronicles

The Chronicle PDFs are image-based. Their rendered page images can be inspected and OCR-processed; they are not to be classified as unreadable merely because native PDF text extraction is empty.

Chronicle statements become canon only after source extraction and harmonization.

### C-007 — Aurelian Helios

True age: **36 years**.

### C-008 — Linus

`Inneres Feuer` is metaphorical, describing inner character strength rather than literal fire power.

Canonical visual symbol: **Aquamarin-Sternkristall**.
