## Current structural rule

Mirathia is the central biome and resonance heart. The seven outer biomes retain their regional relationship to the center.

## Evidence status

The eight biome records in Airtable are currently marked **Kanon**. Their structured descriptions identify the biome, Urbestie, Artefakt, resonance and ruler.

## Important source distinction

Older YesWriter material contains additional regional descriptions and some older naming variants. Those details are not silently overwritten here. They must be harmonized against the current structured canon before promotion.
