## Individual anchors

### Aurelian Helios
- First of the High Council.
- Guardian of balance and justice.
- Central ensemble anchor.
- Solar magic.
- Character core: justice, discipline, fatherly kindness, responsibility.
- Equipment: Sonnenstahl-Rüstung; Auge von Mirathia.
- Plot: centre of final defence; connects the other sectors.

### Theron Tiefenquell
- King of Aquarelis.
- Guardian of currents; defender.
- Water magic.
- Character core: loyalty, patience, constant protection.
- Equipment: Seedrachen-Schuppenrüstung; Saphir der Ewigen Flut.
- Plot: deflects shadow attacks and protects the council chamber with water walls.

### Sylva Moosherz
- Regent of flora; healer of hearts; guardian of Sylvandor.
- Flora, healing and life.
- Mentor to Sunny.
- Character core: empathy, patience, growth, peace orientation.
- Equipment: Welteneschenkranz; Uressenz des Lebens.
- Plot: secures the stone table and closes magical wounds.

### Mira Glimmerstrom
- Mistress of illusions; guardian of hidden paths; weaver of veils.
- Illusion magic, clairvoyance and spatial curvature.
- Character core: analysis, mystery and protection through deception.
- Equipment: Ewiger Glimmer-Spiegel; Nebelseide.
- Plot: hides the true ritual location in the finale.

### Altair Frostspitze
- King of Aethyrspitz; guardian of order; tactician.
- Cryokinesis, aeromancy and crystalline analysis.
- Character core: duty, precision, order, strategic clarity.
- Equipment: Eiseneis-Rüstung; Splitter des Ewigen Eises.
- Plot: analyses attack waves and coordinates activation.

### Pyrak Brennstein
- Master smith of Pyrakh; lord of the forge; guardian of eternal fire.
- Pyrokinesis, magical smithing and heat resonance.
- Character core: passion, courage, honour, creative fire.
- Equipment: Obsidian-/Vulkanglasrüstung; Glutkern der Erde; Feuerdrachenmantel.
- Plot: counterattack and smith of the final weapon.
- Visual principle: fire -> heat -> transformation -> creation -> protection.

### Argentos Silberblick
- Lord of Lunafall; keeper of the Golden Expanse; keeper of courage to live.
- Moonlight structure magic, growth alchemy, cleansing and regeneration.
- Character core: patience, optimism, provision, community.
- Equipment: Mond-Silber-Sichel; Sichelmond-Samen.
- Plot: regenerates the strength and courage of defenders in the finale.

### Vespera Nebelschleier
- Lady of Vesperia; weaver of dreams; guardian of sleep; seer.
- Oniromancy, starlight manipulation, emotional echoes.
- Character core: empathy, dream understanding, psychological depth.
- Equipment: Traumgespinst; Phiole der Sternentränen.
- Plot: protects the children mentally and maintains the dream connection in the finale.

## 8-page production dossier
### Page 0 — Cover
Monumental group composition. Aurelian exactly in the centre; the other seven form a harmonious circle. Individual gemstone/colour identities remain visible and distinct.

### Page 1 — Facts
Uniform character modules: name, title, age/effect, origin/realm, role, magic/core power, artefact/equipment, character core, strengths, weaknesses, development, relationships, plot function, symbol.

### Page 2 — Lore & Background
Illustrated chronology of origin, calling, relationships, trials, conflicts and historical significance. Use only confirmed canon; mark unresolved points as open.

### Page 3 — Hierarchy
Light order with the eight Watchers and documented child/main-role relationships. Shadow order with Irdra, Morwen, Voros, Grothak and Malakor.

### Page 4 — Concept Variants
Existing design development only: silhouette, face, eyes, hair, clothing/armour, accessories, weapons, magic, symbols and colour/gem signature. Do not invent new variants.

### Page 5 — Scenes
Cinematic studies: group scene, interaction, trial/conflict, light vs shadow, quiet moment, epic climax. Confirmed plot only; free compositions explicitly treated as concept studies.

### Page 6 — Quotes & Philosophy
Eight individual sections with confirmed original quotes, philosophy, values, inner conflicts and relationship to the Light. Never invent quotations.

### Page 7 — Closing
Iconic shared image with group function, individual development, shadow relationship, meaning for Mirathia and open points.
